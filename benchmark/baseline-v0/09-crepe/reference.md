# Reference — Crepe

## Ingredients observed
- Flour
- Eggs
- Sugar
- Salt
- Milk
- Butter (melted)
- Oil (for greasing the pan)

## Explicit quantities
- Flour: 250 g
- Eggs: 4
- Sugar: 40 g
- Salt: 1 pinch (1 pincée)
- Milk: 60 cl
- Butter (melted): 40 g
- Oil (for pan): quantity unknown

## Tools
- Large mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Ladle
- Frying pan / Crêpe pan
- Spatula

## Major actions / steps
1. Add 250g flour, 4 eggs, 40g sugar, and 1 pinch of salt to a mixing bowl.
2. Begin whisking the dry ingredients and eggs together.
3. Gradually pour in 60 cl of milk while whisking continuously.
4. Pour in 40g of melted butter and continue whisking until the batter is completely smooth and lump-free.
5. Cover/rest the batter for 45 minutes.
6. Heat a crêpe pan over high heat and lightly oil it.
7. Ladle a portion of batter into the pan, swirl to coat the bottom, and cook until golden on both sides (flipping midway).
8. (Optional) Serve folded with toppings such as chocolate hazelnut spread, crushed nuts, or sugar.

## Explicit times / temperatures
- Batter rest time: 45 minutes
- Pan temperature: High heat / hot pan ("bien chaude")

## Important visual-only information
- Melted butter is incorporated directly into the liquid batter during mixing.
- A hand whisk is used exclusively to achieve a lump-free texture.
- Crêpes are flipped in the pan and plated folded into quarters.

## Notes
- Based on chef Philippe Etchebest's crêpe recipe as recreated in French ("Recette crêpes inratables de Philippe Etchebest").
