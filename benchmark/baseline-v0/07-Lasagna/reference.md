# Reference — Lasagna

- Olive oil
- Smoked pancetta
- Carrots
- Celery
- Red onions
- Garlic
- Bay leaves
- Fresh rosemary

- Cheddar cheese
- Parmesan cheese
- Smoked mozzarella
- Fresh lasagne sheets

## Explicit quantities
- Nutmeg: ~1/2 nutmeg (grated)
- Red wine: 2 small glasses
- Tinned lentils: 2 tins
- Tinned plum tomatoes: 2 tins (plus 2 tins of water)
- Butter: 50 g
- Leek: 1 leek
- Plain flour: 4 medium heaped tbsp
- Milk: 1 litre
- Smoked pancetta, minced beef, minced pork, vegetables, cheeses: quantities unstated

## Tools
- Large pan / pot (for ragu)
- Saucepan (for white sauce)
- Hand immersion blender (for white sauce)
- Large ovenproof baking dish
- Knife
- Cutting board
- Grater (for cheeses and nutmeg)
- Wooden spoon
- Ladle

## Major actions / steps
1. Sauté finely sliced smoked pancetta in olive oil, then add garlic, bay leaves, and chopped rosemary.
2. Add chopped carrots, celery, and red onions, cooking for ~5 minutes.
3. Push vegetables to one side of the pan, add minced pork and beef, break apart, and brown for ~10 minutes.
4. Season ragu with salt, generous black pepper, and grate in ~1/2 nutmeg.
5. Pour in 2 small glasses of red wine, add tinned lentils, tinned plum tomatoes, and 2 tins of water.
6. Simmer ragu uncovered for 1.5 hours until thick and flavorful.
7. Melt 50g butter in a saucepan, add 1 sliced leek, and cook gently.
8. Stir in 4 heaped tbsp plain flour to form a paste, then gradually whisk/stir in 1 litre milk and bring to a simmer.
9. Tilt the saucepan and blend white sauce smooth using an immersion blender.
10. Grate Cheddar, Parmesan, and Smoked Mozzarella directly into the warm white sauce, stirring until melted and stringy.
11. Check ragu consistency; stir in a splash of water if it has dried out too much during simmering.
12. Assemble in baking dish: ladle ragu, cover with fresh lasagne sheets, then pour over leek cheese sauce. Repeat layers.
13. Finish top layer with white sauce and extra grated cheese.
14. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40–45 minutes until bubbling and golden brown.
15. Let rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing.

## Explicit times / temperatures
- Veggie sauté time: ~5 minutes
- Meat browning time: ~10 minutes
- Ragu simmer time: 1.5 hours (about 90 minutes)
- Baking temperature: 180°C (350°F)
- Baking time: 40–45 minutes
- Post-bake resting time: 5–10 minutes

## Important visual-only information
- Immersion blender is used directly inside the tilted saucepan to blend cooked leeks into the sauce base.
- All three cheeses (including smoked mozzarella) are melted directly into the béchamel sauce rather than layered raw.
- Fresh lasagne sheets are laid dry directly into the dish without pre-boiling.

## Notes
- Blending leeks into béchamel, using lentils to stretch the meat ragu, and incorporating smoked mozzarella into the white sauce are key custom features of this recipe.
