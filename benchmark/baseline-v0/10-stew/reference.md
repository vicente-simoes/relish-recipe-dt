# Reference — Pot Beef Stew

## Ingredients observed
- Beef chuck
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Button mushrooms
- Carrots
- Pearl onions
- Butter
- All-purpose flour
- Store-bought beef stock
- Better than Bouillon beef paste
- Tomato paste
- Worcestershire sauce
- Powdered gelatin
- Dark stout beer
- Small golden potatoes
- Bay leaves
- Fresh thyme sprigs
- Frozen peas
- Black pepper
- Crusty sourdough bread

## Explicit quantities
- Beef chuck: 5 lbs (2.5 kg), trimmed to ~3.5 lbs (1.5 kg) cubed meat
- Salt: 2 generous pinches for beef, strong pinch for veggies, extra pinch to adjust final taste
- Button mushrooms: 300 g (quartered)
- Carrots: 400 g (thickly sliced)
- Pearl onions: 200 g
- Butter: 1 stick (115 g)
- All-purpose flour: 100 g (for roux) + 25 g (for coating veggies/potatoes)
- Store-bought beef stock: 2 boxes (1800 g)
- Better than Bouillon: 30 g
- Tomato paste: 30 g
- Worcestershire sauce: 30 g
- Powdered gelatin: 4 packets (28 g)
- Dark stout beer: 1 pint (450 g)
- Small golden potatoes: 500 g
- Bay leaves: 2–3 leaves
- Fresh thyme: 3–4 sprigs
- Frozen peas: 150 g
- Black pepper & crusty bread: for serving

## Tools
- Chef knife & Paring knife
- Vegetable peeler
- Half sheet tray (baking tray)
- Large Dutch oven / heavy-bottomed pot
- Large mixing bowls
- Wooden spoon
- Immersion blender or whisk
- Small ladle
- Fork (for testing doneness)

## Major actions / steps
1. Preheat oven to 500°F (260°C).
2. Trim hard fat bands from beef chuck, cut into 1 to 1.5-inch cubes, toss in a bowl with olive oil and 2 pinches of salt.
3. Spread beef onto a sheet tray and roast in the 500°F oven for 20–25 minutes until deeply browned.
4. Prep veggies: peel pearl onions, thick-slice carrots, and quarter mushrooms.
5. Heat olive oil in Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms, carrots, pearl onions, and salt. Sauté/sweat 3–5 minutes until lightly glazed, then transfer to a bowl.
6. Reduce heat to medium, melt 1 stick butter, add 100g flour, and fry roux for 60–90 seconds while scraping up browned bits.
7. Blend/whisk beef stock, Better than Bouillon, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, and gelatin together.
8. Pour stock liquid and dark stout beer into pot. Bring to a simmer, stirring continuously to incorporate roux.
9. Stir in seared beef. Deglaze sheet tray with a splash of water, scrape up fond, and pour into pot.
10. Lower oven to 300°F (150°C). Place pot UNCOVERED into oven to cook for 2 hours (stirring at 1 hour).
11. Peel and half 500g golden potatoes. Combine with par-cooked veggies in a bowl and toss with 25g flour.
12. Gently stir floured veggies/potatoes, thyme sprigs, and bay leaves into stew.
13. Return pot UNCOVERED to 300°F oven for 1 more hour (3 hours total cook time) until beef is shreddable and potatoes are fork-tender.
14. Skim excess rendered fat from surface using a small ladle.
15. Season with extra salt if needed, then stir in 150g frozen peas off-heat to thaw/warm through.
16. Plate in bowls, top with fresh cracked black pepper, and serve with crusty sourdough bread.

## Explicit times / temperatures
- Oven temp (beef searing): 500°F (260°C)
- Beef oven-searing time: 20–25 minutes
- Veggie sweating time: 3–5 minutes
- Roux cooking time: 60–90 seconds
- Oven temp (stewing): 300°F (150°C)
- First stew phase (meat only): 2 hours (stirred at 1 hour mark)
- Second stew phase (with veggies/potatoes): 1 hour
- Total stew cooking time: 3 hours

## Important visual-only information
- Beef is seared in the oven on a sheet pan rather than in batches on the stovetop.
- Pot remains UNCOVERED during both oven phases to allow evaporation for sauce reduction and top surface browning.
- Powdered gelatin is mixed into boxed stock to replicate the gelatinous body of long-simmered homemade bone broth.
- Flouring the veggies/potatoes before adding them acts as a secondary, lump-free thickener.

## Notes
- Restaurant tip mentioned: Letting stew rest in fridge overnight enhances flavor and solidifies fat on top for effortless removal.
