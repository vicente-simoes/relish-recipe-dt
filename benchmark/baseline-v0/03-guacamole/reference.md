## Tools

- Pestle / Stone tool (for mashing)
- Spoon
- Knife

## Major actions / steps

1. Scoop the avocado flesh into the molcajete.
2. Mash the avocado.
3. Dice the tomatoes, jalapeño, cilantro, and onions.
4. Add the diced vegetables to the mashed avocado.
5. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the mixture.
6. Sprinkle salt over the mixture.
7. Mix all the ingredients together with a spoon.
