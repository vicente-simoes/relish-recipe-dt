# Reference — Tortilla Española

## Ingredients observed
- Potatoes
- Onion
- Eggs
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt

## Explicit quantities
- Large potatoes: 3
- Onion: 1
- Eggs: 4 or 5
- Extra virgin olive oil: quantity unknown (enough to submerge/poach potatoes)
- Salt: quantity unknown

## Tools
- Knife
- Cutting board
- Large frying pan (for poaching potatoes)
- Small frying pan (for cooking tortilla)
- Pan lid
- Slotted skimmer / spider filter
- Large bowl
- Spatula
- Plate (for flipping)

## Major actions / steps
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin rounds.
2. Slice the onion into thin strips.
3. Heat a generous amount of extra virgin olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
4. Add the potatoes and onions to the oil, season with salt, and lower heat to medium-low so they poach without burning.
5. Cover the pan with a lid and cook, gently mashing/breaking up the potatoes occasionally with a spatula.
6. Once cooked through and softened, turn the heat to high briefly to lightly toast/brown the potatoes, then remove from heat.
7. Crack the eggs into a large bowl, add a pinch of salt, and beat well.
8. Drain the cooked potatoes and onions using a slotted skimmer and transfer into the beaten eggs. Mix well.
9. Heat a small splash of the reserved olive oil in a smaller frying pan over medium-low heat.
10. Pour the egg, potato, and onion mixture into the pan and cook for about 5 minutes, shaking the pan occasionally to prevent sticking.
11. Place a flat plate over the pan, quickly invert to flip the tortilla.
12. Add another small drizzle of oil to the pan, then carefully slide the tortilla back in.
13. Tuck the edges inward with a spatula and cook the second side over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes.
14. *(Optional for well-done center)*: If a fully set/well-done tortilla is preferred (*bien cuajada*), flip one more time and cook for an additional 3 minutes.
15. Transfer the finished tortilla to a plate and serve.

## Explicit times / temperatures
- Poaching potatoes: medium-low heat (with high heat briefly at the end)
- Cooking tortilla: medium-low heat
- First side cook time: ~5 minutes
- Second side cook time: 4 to 5 minutes (for soft/runny center)
- Optional extra cook time: additional 3 minutes after a second flip (for well-done center)

## Important visual-only information
- Potatoes and onions are poached/deep-cooked in oil rather than sauteed.
- A slotted spider filter is used to drain excess oil before adding hot potatoes directly into raw eggs.
- The edges are tucked continuously with a spatula to form a smooth, rounded puck shape.
- Doneness is checked by inserting a knife tip into the center.

## Notes
- Onion is explicitly included in this recipe.
- The recipe offers flexibility for doneness preferences (*poco cuajada* vs. *bien cuajada*).
