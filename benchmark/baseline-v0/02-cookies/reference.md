## Explicit quantities

Only include quantities that are actually communicated by speech, captions, or text on screen:

- Unsalted butter: 110g
- Light brown sugar: 150g
- Granulated sugar: 115g
- Large egg: 1
- Vanilla: 1 tsp
- Plain flour: 200g
- Bicarbonate of soda: 1/2 tsp
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Chocolate chips: 200g
- Dough portion: 15g (or about 1 tablespoon)

## Tools

- Bowl
- Whisk
- Spatula
- Cookie sheet (baking tray)

## Major actions / steps

1. Whisk together melted butter, light brown sugar, and granulated sugar until fully combined.
2. Add the egg and vanilla, and whisk them in.
3. Add plain flour, bicarbonate of soda, and salt, and fold them in to form a dough.
4. Fold in the chocolate chips.
5. Scoop up about a tablespoon (15g) of dough and roll it into a ball.
6. Place the dough balls onto a cookie sheet.
7. Bake in the oven at 190°C / 170°C (fan) for 5–6 minutes.
8. Let the cookies cool slightly before serving.
