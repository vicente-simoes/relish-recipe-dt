# Reference — Focaccia

## Ingredients observed
- All-purpose flour
- Warm water
- Sea salt
- Active dry yeast
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Fresh rosemary
- Garlic confit (peeled garlic cloves cooked low & slow in olive oil)
- Garlic-infused olive oil
- Coarse sea salt

## Explicit quantities
- All-purpose flour: 600 g (4 1/2 cups)
- Warm water: 450 g
- Active dry yeast: 1/4 tsp
- Extra virgin olive oil (for dough): 2 tbsp
- Sea salt: quantity unknown
- Olive oil (for pan, confit & post-bake drizzle): quantity unknown
- Fresh rosemary: quantity unknown
- Garlic confit: ~20 cloves
- Coarse sea salt: quantity unknown

## Tools
- Large mixing bowl
- Plastic bench scraper (with rounded edge)
- 9x13 inch baking pan
- Plastic wrap / wet towel
- Wire cooling rack
- Chef knife / Bread knife

## Major actions / steps
1. Whisk together flour and active dry yeast in a large bowl, making a well in the center.
2. Dissolve sea salt in warm water in a separate bowl.
3. Pour warm salted water and 2 tbsp olive oil into the flour well, then mix by hand using a pinching technique until a wet, shaggy dough forms.
4. Transfer dough to a greased bowl using a plastic bench scraper.
5. Perform 3 to 4 stretch-and-fold turns, cover with a wet towel, and rest for 20 minutes. Repeat stretch-and-folds 3 to 4 more times.
6. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and let dough ferment overnight in the refrigerator.
7. Generously oil a 9x13 inch pan with 1 tbsp olive oil and transfer dough into it, gently stretching toward the corners.
8. Cover and let the dough proof for a first pan-proof of 1 hour (ideally in a warm place like an oven with the light on).
9. Wet fingers in water and press deep dimples into the dough from edge to edge.
10. Cover again and let the dough proof a second time in the pan for 45 to 60 minutes until doubled and jiggling.
11. Top with fresh rosemary leaves, ~20 garlic confit cloves (pressed lightly into dough), garlic-infused oil, and coarse sea salt.
12. Bake at 450°F (230°C) for 15 to 20 minutes (until internal temp hits 190°F / 88°C).
13. Remove bread from pan to a wire rack, drizzle with extra virgin olive oil while warm, slice, and serve.

## Explicit times / temperatures
- Water temperature: 102°F–105°F (39°C–40°C)
- Rest interval between fold sets: ~20 minutes
- First fermentation: Overnight in refrigerator
- Pan proof #1: 1 hour
- Pan proof #2 (after dimpling): 45–60 minutes
- Ambient proofing temperature: 80°F–90°F (27°C–32°C) or oven with light on
- Baking temperature: 450°F (230°C)
- Baking time: 15–20 minutes
- Internal target temperature: 190°F (88°C)

## Important visual-only information
- Uses a thumb-and-finger pinching method rather than traditional kneading.
- Rounded bench scraper helps clean sticky, high-hydration (75%) dough off bowl walls.
- Wetting hands before dimpling prevents dough from sticking to fingers and deflating.
- Finished focaccia has an airy, springy crumb with small-to-medium air pockets and a crispy, oil-fried bottom crust.

## Notes
- Stand mixer with a dough hook is mentioned as an option.
- Garlic confit is prepared by cooking garlic cloves submerged in olive oil over very low heat for 30 minutes.
