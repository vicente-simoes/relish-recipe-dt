## Major actions / steps

1. Bring the stock to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a simmer to keep it hot throughout cooking.
2. Heat olive oil in a separate pot over medium heat and sauté diced shallots until translucent.
3. Add sliced shiitake mushrooms and butter; cook until mushrooms are softened and tender.
4. Add garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper, and stir until aromatic.
5. Add Arborio rice and stir for 1–2 minutes to coat in oil and lightly toast the grains.
6. Pour in a splash of white wine (or lemon juice) and cook until the liquid has evaporated.
7. Gradually add hot stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently until absorbed before adding the next ladle. Continue for 15–20 minutes until rice is al dente.
8. Remove from heat when the rice is creamy and passes the doneness test (spoon dragged through slowly fills back in).
9. Stir in grated Parmesan cheese until fully incorporated.
10. Plate and garnish with chopped fresh parsley, extra grated Parmesan, and cracked black pepper.
