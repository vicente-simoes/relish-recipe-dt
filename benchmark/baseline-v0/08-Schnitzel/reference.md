# Reference — Chicken Schnitzel

## Ingredients observed
- Chicken breast
- Flour
- Eggs
- Breadcrumbs
- Cooking oil / butter (for frying)
- Lemon wedges (for serving)
- French fries / chips (for serving side)

## Explicit quantities
- Chicken breast: 1 piece
- Eggs: 2
- Flour: quantity unknown
- Breadcrumbs: quantity unknown
- Cooking oil / butter: quantity unknown
- Lemon: 2 wedges
- French fries: 1 side portion

## Tools
- Meat mallet
- Plastic wrap (placed over meat while pounding)
- Cutting board
- 1 glass baking dish (for eggs)
- 2 shallow round plates (for flour and breadcrumbs)
- Frying pan / skillet
- Metal tongs

## Major actions / steps
1. Place the chicken breast on a cutting board, cover it with a layer of plastic wrap, and pound it flat with a meat mallet.
2. Dredge the flattened chicken in flour until fully coated, shaking off any excess.
3. Crack 2 eggs into a dish, beat them, and coat the floured chicken in the egg wash.
4. Transfer the chicken into breadcrumbs, pressing firmly on both sides to adhere a thick coating.
5. Shallow-fry the breaded schnitzel in hot oil/butter in a pan, flipping with tongs until both sides are golden brown.
6. Serve hot on a plate with french fries and lemon wedges.
